Computational Linguistic Principles
1. Power-Law Vocabulary Distribution (Zipf's Law)

Natural language exhibits an inverse power-law frequency distribution. Stopword filtering attenuates dominant functional terms to expose discriminative topical keywords.

2. Collocation Extraction & Compound Phrasing

Multi-word expressions like "machine learning" or "attention mechanism" convey composite semantic units that preserve meaning beyond bag-of-words assumptions.

3. Graph Centrality & Thematic Core Extraction

Terms exhibiting high degree and eigenvector centrality in the co-occurrence network form the foundational thematic pillars of the underlying text corpus.
